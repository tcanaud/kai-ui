Two layout issues affecting panel usability across viewport sizes:

**FB-036** — On mobile, the terminal panel takes up the full viewport height, pushing all other panels entirely below the fold. There is no scroll hint or indicator that more content exists below.

**FB-037** — On desktop, the panel grid does not stretch to fill the full viewport height. A blank dark area is visible below the panels, making the layout look unfinished. (`panel-layout.tsx`)

**Fixes**:
- Mobile: cap terminal panel height (e.g., `max-h-[50vh]`) and add a scroll shadow/gradient hint at the bottom of the panel list.
- Desktop: apply `h-full` or `min-h-screen` to the panel grid container with `grid-rows-[1fr]` so the last row expands to fill remaining space. Alternatively use `flex-1` on the panel area wrapper.

**Acceptance criteria**:
- On mobile, multiple panels are visible in the viewport simultaneously without scrolling past the terminal.
- A visual scroll hint indicates overflow content when present.
- On desktop, panels stretch to fill the full viewport height with no blank area below.
