The sidebar session list renders `aborted` and `completed` sessions with no visual distinction between the two states. Users cannot tell at a glance whether a session ended normally or was interrupted.

**Current behavior**: Both statuses appear identical in the sidebar — same color, icon, and text treatment.

**Expected behavior**: `aborted` sessions should have a distinct visual indicator (e.g., a warning-colored dot or strikethrough treatment) to differentiate them from cleanly `completed` sessions.

**File**: `session-item.tsx` — status badge/indicator rendering logic.

**Fix**:
- Add a status-to-style map covering `completed`, `aborted`, `running`, `pending`
- Use distinct colors: e.g., green for completed, amber/red for aborted
- Optionally add an icon (checkmark vs. X or warning triangle)
