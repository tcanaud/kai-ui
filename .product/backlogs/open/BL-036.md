The playbook selection list in the Create Session dialog includes internal/template entries such as `autohotfix-old` and `playbook.tpl` that should not be visible to end users.

**Fix**: Filter the playbook list to exclude:
- Entries ending in `.tpl` (template files)
- Entries with `-old` suffix (deprecated/archived playbooks)
- Entries starting with `_` (convention for hidden/internal items)

Consider formalizing the naming convention so filtering rules are explicit and maintainable (e.g., prefix internal playbooks with `_`).

**Acceptance criteria**:
- `autohotfix-old` and `playbook.tpl` do not appear in the Create Session playbook picker.
- Valid user-facing playbooks are unaffected.
- The filtering rule is documented or enforced by convention.
