Two performance findings from Chrome DevTools performance tracing that impact initial page load:

1. **FB-016 — LCP 3,729ms**: Largest Contentful Paint is in the "Needs Improvement" range (> 2,500ms). 98.5% of delay is render delay from JS bundle/hydration cost. Investigate: lazy-loading xterm.js via `next/dynamic` with `ssr: false`, code-splitting heavy chunks, and profiling hydration cost in React DevTools.

2. **FB-017 — Geist font in critical path (331ms)**: The Geist WOFF2 font loads in the critical rendering path, contributing to flash of unstyled text. Add `<link rel="preload" as="font">` or verify `next/font` is configured with preloading and `display: "swap"`.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- LCP improves to < 2,500ms on a standard development machine
- Geist font is preloaded and does not block text rendering
- xterm.js is lazy-loaded and does not contribute to initial bundle parse cost
