On mobile, closing the new-session dialog (opened from within the navigation sheet) causes focus to fall to `document.body` instead of returning to the hamburger trigger button.

**Root cause**: A race condition between the Sheet and Dialog unmount animations. When both close simultaneously, Radix UI's focus-return logic loses track of the original trigger element.

**Reproduction**:
1. Open the app on a mobile viewport.
2. Open the hamburger navigation sheet.
3. Tap "New Session" to open the dialog.
4. Close the dialog (cancel or submit).
5. Focus is now on `document.body`, not the hamburger button.

**Fix**: Use a `onCloseAutoFocus` callback on the Dialog to manually restore focus to the hamburger button ref, or track the trigger ref explicitly and call `.focus()` after the dialog close animation completes.

This is a regression introduced by the focus management changes in feature-007 (keyboard accessibility fix).
