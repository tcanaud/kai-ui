**Severity**: Critical (intermittent)

**Reproduction Steps**:
1. Open the app at viewport 375x812 (mobile)
2. Tap the hamburger menu to open the sidebar Sheet overlay
3. Attempt to close via: (a) Close (X) button, (b) clicking the backdrop/overlay, (c) pressing Escape
4. Observe the `data-state` attribute on the sheet-content and sheet-overlay elements

**Expected**: The sidebar sheet closes via any of the three standard dismiss mechanisms
**Actual**: All three dismiss methods fail — `data-state` remains "open". The only way to dismiss is to reload the page. Found failing in step 2 of the QA session but working in step 6 — behaviour is intermittent.

**Notes**: The `Sheet` in `src/app/components/layout/mobile-nav.tsx` is used in uncontrolled mode (no `open`/`onOpenChange` props). Investigate the Radix UI Dialog primitive used as `SheetPrimitive` in `src/components/ui/sheet.tsx`. Consider switching to controlled state.

**Source**: QA session — manual exploratory testing at 375x812. Test criterion: US5.AC3.
