**Severity**: Medium

**Reproduction Steps**:
1. Open the app at mobile viewport (375x812)
2. Inspect the hamburger menu button with a screen reader or accessibility tool

**Expected**: Screen reader announces something like "Open session sidebar, button"
**Actual**: Screen reader announces "button" with no indication of purpose. The `Button` in `src/app/components/layout/mobile-nav.tsx` has no `aria-label`, no `title` attribute, no visible text, and no `sr-only` child.

**Fix Suggestion**: Add `aria-label="Open session sidebar"` or a `<span className="sr-only">Open sidebar</span>` child to the hamburger `Button` in `MobileNav`.

**Source**: QA session — accessibility audit on mobile 375x812.
