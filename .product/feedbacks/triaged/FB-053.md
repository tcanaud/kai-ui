The `SelectTrigger` component used for the playbook selector has no accessible name. Because the `Label` association is broken (see FB-048), and no `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` is set directly on the trigger, screen readers announce it without any label — typically just "combobox" or "button".

Fix: Once the label association is fixed (FB-048), this issue should resolve automatically. As a standalone fix, add `aria-label="Playbook"` directly to the `SelectTrigger` component until the label `id`/`htmlFor` pairing is corrected.
