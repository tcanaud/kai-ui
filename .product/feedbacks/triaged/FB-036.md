**Severity**: LOW

On mobile viewports, the Terminal panel takes up the full viewport height, pushing all other panels completely below the fold. There is no visual scroll indicator or hint that more content exists below.

**Fix**:
1. Cap the terminal panel height on mobile (e.g., `max-h-[50vh]` or `max-h-64`) to leave room for other panels.
2. Add a scroll shadow or gradient hint at the bottom of the panel list to indicate overflow content.
3. Consider a mobile-optimized layout with collapsible panels or a tab-based switcher.
