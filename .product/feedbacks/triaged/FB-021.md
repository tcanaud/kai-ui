**Observation**: Clicking an already-active panel deselects it — the neon cyan glow border is removed and `activePanel` returns to `null`. A double-click also deselects without any additional action. There is no dedicated "deselect all" affordance, and no visual cue that toggle-deselect is the intended behaviour.

Users may accidentally deselect a panel without realising it, or may wonder whether clicking the active panel again is supposed to do something meaningful (e.g., expand, focus, or maximize it).

**Suggestion**: Decide whether panel deselection via re-click is intentional. If yes, add a subtle visual cue (tooltip or brief animation) so users understand the interaction. If no, remove the toggle behaviour so clicking an active panel keeps it selected.

**Source**: QA session — visual integration test. Test criterion: US4.AC3.
