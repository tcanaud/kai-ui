**Severity**: MINOR

When all panels are toggled off, the main content area shows a blank/empty state with no guidance. Users may be confused about why the content area is empty and how to restore a panel.

**Fix**: Display an empty-state message in the main content area when no panel is active. For example: "No panel selected. Click a panel button above to display it." Include visual affordance pointing to the panel toggle controls.
