On mobile, when the new-session dialog is opened from within the navigation sheet and then closed, focus falls to `document.body` instead of returning to the trigger element. This is caused by a race condition between the Sheet and Dialog unmount animations: when both close simultaneously, Radix UI's focus-return logic loses track of the original trigger.

Reproduction:
1. Open the app on a mobile viewport.
2. Open the hamburger navigation sheet.
3. Tap "New Session" to open the dialog.
4. Close the dialog (cancel or submit).
5. Observe focus is now on `document.body`, not the hamburger button.

Fix: Manually restore focus to the hamburger button (or another appropriate element) using a `onCloseAutoFocus` callback on the Dialog, or by tracking the trigger ref and calling `.focus()` after the dialog close animation completes.
