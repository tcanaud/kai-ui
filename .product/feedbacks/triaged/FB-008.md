**Severity**: Medium

**Reproduction Steps**:
1. Open the app at viewport 768x1024 (tablet)
2. Observe the layout

**Expected**: The sidebar should collapse into a hamburger/sheet navigation at tablet widths, or the panel grid should reflow to fewer columns to remain usable
**Actual**: The full desktop sidebar (~270px) is rendered with no hamburger menu. This leaves only ~500px for the main panel grid. The 3-column panel grid becomes visually cramped — panels shrink to ~160px wide each. No breakpoint transition exists between desktop sidebar and hamburger/sheet navigation at 768px.

**Fix Suggestion**: Add a `md` breakpoint (e.g., Tailwind `md:hidden`) to collapse the sidebar into a sheet/hamburger on tablet viewports (≤ 1024px or ≤ 768px), or ensure the panel grid reflows to 2-column or 1-column at tablet widths.

**Source**: QA session — responsive layout test at 768x1024. Test criterion: US5.AC3.
