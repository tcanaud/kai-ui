At 768px (tablet), the app uses the same single-column mobile layout as on narrow phones. No intermediate breakpoint exists between mobile and desktop, resulting in significant wasted horizontal space on tablet-sized screens where a sidebar or split layout would be appropriate.

Fix: Introduce a tablet breakpoint (e.g. `md` in Tailwind, 768px) that shows a compact sidebar alongside the main content area, taking advantage of the available horizontal space without requiring the full desktop layout.
