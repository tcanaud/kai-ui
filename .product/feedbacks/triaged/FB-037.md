**Severity**: MEDIUM

On desktop, the panel grid does not stretch to fill the full viewport height. A blank dark area is visible below the panels, making the layout feel unfinished.

**File**: `panel-layout.tsx`

**Fix**: Apply `h-full` or `min-h-screen` to the panel grid container and ensure grid rows use `auto` sizing that allows the last row to expand. In Tailwind: `grid-rows-[1fr]` on the container with `h-full` to make panels fill remaining space. Alternatively use `flex-1` on the panel area wrapper.
