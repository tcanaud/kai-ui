Panel divs with `role="button"` do not apply `cursor: pointer`. Unlike native `<button>` elements, divs with `role="button"` do not inherit pointer cursor styling by default. Users have no visual affordance indicating the panels are clickable.

Fix: Add `cursor-pointer` (Tailwind) or `cursor: pointer` (CSS) to the panel container elements that have `role="button"`. This should be applied consistently across all interactive panel headers/containers.
