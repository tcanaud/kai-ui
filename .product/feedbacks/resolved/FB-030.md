**Severity**: MEDIUM

In `session-sidebar.tsx`, focused and selected session buttons use the same cyan border styling. Keyboard users cannot tell whether they are looking at the currently active session or simply hovering/focusing a different one.

**File**: `session-sidebar.tsx` — session button focus and selected styles.

**WCAG violation**: 1.4.1 Use of Color (Level A) — color alone must not be the only means of conveying information.

**Fix**: Differentiate the focus ring from the selected state visually. For example, use a dashed or double outline for focus while keeping a solid border for selection. Alternatively, use different colors (e.g., white focus ring vs. cyan selection border).
