The xterm terminal renders corrupted or garbled characters on all tested viewport sizes. This is caused by xterm's character measurement overflowing its container, resulting in incorrect character cell dimensions. The fit addon likely computes wrong column/row counts when the terminal container dimensions are not correctly constrained.

Reproduction:
1. Open a session with an active terminal.
2. Observe garbled/overlapping characters in the terminal output across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.

Fix: Ensure the terminal container has explicit, bounded dimensions before the fit addon is applied. Call `fitAddon.fit()` only after the container is fully laid out (e.g. in a `ResizeObserver` callback or after a layout paint). Verify `xterm-screen` does not overflow its parent grid cell.
