The `xterm-screen` element overflows its assigned grid cell and extends over adjacent panel areas, including the Playbook panel header. This makes the Playbook panel header unclickable because pointer events are intercepted by the overflowing terminal layer.

Root cause: `PanelSlot` is missing `h-full` (or equivalent height constraint), allowing xterm to grow beyond its allocated space.

Reproduction:
1. Open a session with both Terminal and Playbook panels visible.
2. Attempt to click the Playbook panel header to expand/collapse it.
3. Observe that clicks are intercepted and the panel does not respond.

Fix: Add `h-full` (and `overflow-hidden`) to the `PanelSlot` container so xterm is constrained to its grid cell and cannot overflow into adjacent areas.
