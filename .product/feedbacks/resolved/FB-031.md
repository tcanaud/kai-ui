**Severity**: MEDIUM

The "New Session" button in `session-sidebar.tsx` uses `outline:none` and only a faint `box-shadow` glow as its focus indicator. This likely fails WCAG 2.4.7 Focus Visible (Level AA), which requires keyboard focus to be visually visible.

**File**: `session-sidebar.tsx` — "New Session" button focus styles.

**WCAG reference**: 2.4.7 Focus Visible (Level AA).

**Fix**: Replace `outline:none` with a visible focus ring. Use `focus-visible:outline` with sufficient contrast (3:1 minimum against adjacent colors per WCAG 2.4.11). Tailwind's `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-white` or similar is appropriate in this dark theme.
