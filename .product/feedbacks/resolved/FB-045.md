The hamburger menu button in `mobile-nav.tsx` has no `aria-label` attribute. Screen readers will announce it without a meaningful name (likely as "button"), making it impossible for assistive technology users to understand its purpose.

Fix: Add `aria-label="Open navigation menu"` (or equivalent) to the button element. Consider also toggling it to `aria-label="Close navigation menu"` when the sheet is open, and setting `aria-expanded` accordingly.
